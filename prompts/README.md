# Exploration prompts

These prompts are conversation starters for research and design work. They are
not production prompts and do not prescribe a particular model or system.

## Learning problem

- What is the learner trying to accomplish, and what currently prevents it?
- At what moment in learning a piece would assistance be most valuable?
- What could a good teacher observe here that the proposed system cannot?
- What would make the output actionable rather than merely informative?

## Evidence and reasoning

- Which conclusions follow from the score, and which depend on the individual?
- What additional evidence would reduce uncertainty?
- How could the system communicate competing explanations instead of inventing
  false certainty?
- What provenance should accompany a recommendation?

## Pedagogy

- Which pedagogical principle or teaching practice supports this idea?
- For whom might the suggestion be suitable or unsuitable?
- What is the intended learning effect, and how could it be observed?
- How does the learner return from an exercise or simplification to the music?
- Where should a teacher remain in control?

## Prototype design

- What is the smallest prototype that could test the central assumption?
- What can be deterministic, and where might an AI component add value?
- What baseline would reveal whether the AI component contributes anything?
- What failure would make the prototype unhelpful or unsafe?

## Evaluation

- Who is qualified to judge this output, and according to which criteria?
- Can usefulness be assessed independently from polished wording?
- What comparison condition would make the result meaningful?
- What evidence can realistically be collected within a master's thesis?

## Company collaboration

- Which part is a research contribution and which part is product development?
- What data, code, or knowledge is proprietary or sensitive?
- Which prototype decisions need to reflect the company's actual users?
- Can the thesis describe and reproduce its findings without exposing company
  material?
