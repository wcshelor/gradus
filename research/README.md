# Research areas

This directory groups substantial research work by purpose.

- `piano_difficulty/` is the established symbolic score-analysis subproject.
- `learning_support/` is the staging area for ways of helping a pianist
  understand, practise, and learn a piece. Its first concrete program,
  `learning_support/passage_practice/`, plans a traceable path from difficulty
  hotspots to passage diagnoses and generated practice exercises.
- `shared/` is reserved for proven cross-area contracts and utilities.

New research areas should be introduced only when they have a distinct question
or experimental boundary. A brainstorm topic does not need its own research
area.
