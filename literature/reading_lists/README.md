# Reading lists

Current maps:

- [`learning_piano_pieces.md`](learning_piano_pieces.md) — what makes a piece or
  passage difficult, how that can be diagnosed, and how teaching or practice
  can respond;
- [`historical_pedagogy_and_etudes.md`](historical_pedagogy_and_etudes.md) —
  classical keyboard treatises, the development and analysis of the étude, and
  criteria for effective pedagogical studies;
- [`ai_assisted_instrument_learning.md`](ai_assisted_instrument_learning.md) —
  state of the art in AI-assisted piano and instrumental learning, including
  automatic performance assessment, commercial apps, personalization,
  generation, multimodal feedback, and evaluation gaps;
- [`thesis_landscape.md`](thesis_landscape.md) — broad thesis territory.

Reading lists are curated, ordered pathways through the literature rather than
duplicates of the tagged source catalog. A paper may appear in several lists or
themes.

Create lists around questions or themes, for example:

- piano pedagogy and practice;
- music difficulty and technical demands;
- intelligent tutoring and learning support;
- generative systems for music education;
- learner modelling and personalization;
- human–AI interaction, explainability, and trust;
- evaluation methods for educational systems.

Use `thesis_landscape.md` as the broad starting list. Define search themes in
`../themes/`, and add focused reading lists when ordering or commentary adds
something beyond filtering the catalog.
