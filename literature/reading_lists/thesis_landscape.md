# Thesis landscape

This is a starting map, not a finalized syllabus. Add sources only after they
have a catalog entry in the `Sources` sheet of
`../bibliography/library.xlsx`.

## Piano learning and pedagogy

Questions to explore:

- How do pianists and teachers identify and work through difficult passages?
- Which practice strategies have empirical or expert support?
- How do technical, musical, cognitive, and motivational needs interact?

## Computational understanding of piano scores

Questions to explore:

- Which properties of a score can be extracted reliably?
- How are difficulty, technical demand, and playability represented?
- What are the limits of score-only inference about an individual learner?

## AI-assisted and technology-assisted learning

Questions to explore:

- Which roles can an automated system usefully play during learning?
- What distinguishes actionable support from fluent but generic advice?
- Which existing intelligent tutoring or music-learning systems are relevant?

Start with `ai_assisted_instrument_learning.md` for the focused state-of-the-art
map. Current systems are strongest at automatic performance assessment and
instant correctness feedback, while diagnosis, intervention matching,
teacher-facing support, and generated piano-technique exercises remain open
research gaps.

## Personalization and learner context

Questions to explore:

- What information is needed to adapt assistance responsibly?
- How can learner input, teacher input, or performance evidence complement the
  score?
- What are appropriate boundaries for automated adaptation?

## Generative and interactive possibilities

Questions to explore:

- What kinds of explanations, representations, exercises, or plans might be
  generated?
- Which outputs require deterministic validation or human review?
- When does generation add value over retrieval, rules, or templates?

## Evaluation and responsible design

Questions to explore:

- How should pedagogical correctness and usefulness be evaluated?
- What can expert review establish, and what requires a learner study?
- How should uncertainty, provenance, privacy, and potential harm be handled?
